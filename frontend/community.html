Common Scam Patterns

Reference examples

These are illustrative examples of well-known scam tactics — not user submissions — shared to help you recognize similar patterns.

Advance-fee / Inheritance scam

Messages claiming you're the beneficiary of unclaimed funds or an inheritance, asking for a "processing fee" before releasing a large sum. Real institutions never ask you to pay to receive money.

Fake delivery / customs fee

A text or email claims your package is held at customs and needs a small fee to release. Legitimate couriers bill through official invoices, not surprise payment links.

Work-from-home job scam

A job offer promising high weekly pay for remote work, requiring a "registration" or "training" deposit up front. Real employers never charge you to start working.

Brand/domain impersonation

Emails from misspelled lookalike domains (e.g. "microsaft.com" instead of "microsoft.com") pretending to be a trusted company's support team.
