🛡️ Safety Tips

Practical guidance to help you spot scams before they reach your wallet.

🚩 Red flags to watch for

  • Urgency and pressure — "Act NOW", "expires in 1 hour". Legitimate companies never rush you.
  • Prizes you didn't enter — You cannot win a lottery you didn't buy a ticket for.
  • Gift card or Bitcoin payments — No real company asks for gift cards or crypto as payment.
  • Shortened URLs — bit.ly, tinyurl hide where they actually go.
  • Requests for OTP or password — Your bank will NEVER ask for your OTP or PIN.
  • Misspelled domains — paypa1.com, amaz0n.com are common fakes.

✅ Safe practices

  • Verify before you act — Call the number on the back of your card, not the one in the message.
  • Use Veridict first — Paste any suspicious content before clicking, replying, or sending anything.
  • Enable two-factor authentication — On banking apps, email, and social media.
  • Tell someone — Scammers rely on embarrassment. Awareness protects everyone.
