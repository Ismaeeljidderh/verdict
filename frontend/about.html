Our Mission

Scams cost Nigerians and people worldwide billions every year — through fake bank alerts, phishing links, impersonation calls, and fraudulent job offers. Veridict exists to put AI-powered scam detection directly in the hands of everyday people, for free, so anyone can verify a suspicious message before it costs them money or peace of mind.

What We Believe

  • Security tools should be free and accessible — not locked behind enterprise pricing.
  • AI should protect people, not just corporations.
  • Nigerian-specific scam patterns deserve Nigerian-built solutions.
  • Community reporting makes everyone safer, together.
