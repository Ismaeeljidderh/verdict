Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

What We Collect

We collect your name, email, username, and password (hashed) when you register. We store your scan history and results to power your dashboard and alerts.

What We Don't Do

  • We never sell your data to third parties.
  • We never share scan content publicly without your explicit consent (Community Reports are opt-in).
  • We don't use your data to train external AI models without anonymization.

Scan Content

Content you paste into scanners is analyzed in real-time and stored in your personal scan history so you can review it later. Only you (and admins, for support purposes) can see your full scan history.

Third-Party Services

We use Paystack for payment processing and Google for optional sign-in. These providers have their own privacy policies governing data they process.

Your Rights

You can update your profile, download your scan history, or permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings.
